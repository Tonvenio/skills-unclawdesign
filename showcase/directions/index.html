Questions

When does my first bag arrive?

Order by Sunday night and your coffee is roasted on Monday and shipped by Wednesday.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Skip, pause or cancel from your account any time up to the Sunday before roasting.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longest. No grinder? Choose your brew method and we grind to match the morning it ships.

How fresh is fresh?

Most coffees taste best from about four days to four weeks after roasting. Your bag arrives at the start of that window.
