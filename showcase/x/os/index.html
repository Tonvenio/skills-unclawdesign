Every month we roast two small-lot coffees, ship them within 48 hours of roasting and include notes on who grew them.     Questions

    When will it arrive?

    We roast batch 041 on Monday 12 October and ship it by Wednesday 14 October. Every bag leaves within 48 hours of roasting.

    Will it fit my letterbox?

    Yes. Each 250 g bag is a resealable valve bag that fits through the letterbox, so nobody needs to be home.

    What comes with it?

    Notes on the farm and the people who grew the coffee, and a recipe.

    One bag or two?

    One bag is 250 g for €14 a month and alternates between the two coffees. Two bags is 2 × 250 g for €26 a month: both coffees, to taste side by side.

    Can I get it ground?

    Whole bean, or ground for filter, espresso, moka pot or French press. The Brew Calculator works out the dose for each.

    Can I skip or cancel?

    Yes. Skip a month, pause or cancel up to the Sunday before roasting. For batch 041 that is Sunday 11 October.

    What’s next?

    The next two lots are announced on 2 November.

    Is Tidewater real?

    No. Tidewater is a fictional roastery, and this desktop is a demo page for the unclawdesign skill. Nothing here takes an order or a payment.
